Personal Projects
Weeki Wachee
2025–2026

Research on mermaiding at the Florida roadside attraction and its ties to tourism, folk museums and the nation-state. The paper Making the Mythical Material: Weeki Wachee, Mermaids, and the Institutionalized Folkloresque was presented at the American Folklore Society Conference in Atlanta, Georgia (October 2025) and UC Berkeley's Folklore Colloquium (December 2025).
