Home Economics
2020–2024
Home Economics was a homemade brand and shop that produced netted bags, clothing, and calendars.

Home Economics was an experimental brand and shop focused on creating netted bottle bags, apparel, and small-run publications.

Built a network of cross-disciplinary collaborations with brands, stores, and creative agencies, including: Bar Part Time (San Francisco), The RealReal (NYC & LA), Tangerine (NYC), Magarchivo (Florence), Lo-Fi (Perth), F. Miller (Toronto), Sunday Drop and EVERYONE (NYC pop-ups), Chickee’s Vintage (NYC), Varyer (Chicago), Birthday Girl/Keely LLC (LA), Post Post Works (NYC), Bobblehaus (NYC), The Corner Market (NYC), and ARCHES (Lisbon).
