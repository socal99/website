Sophia Callahan
Strategy

Sophia Callahan is a freelance insight strategist based in the San Francisco Bay Area. She has written for platforms such as WGSN and has worked with agencies such as TRIPTK, Protein Agency, Irregular Labs, Holiday, Terrane Group, Tropical Futures Institute, and ON ROAD on client projects for Nike, Adidas, Puma, Foot Locker, Tinder, Instagram, The Walton Foundation, Dubai Arts & Culture, W Hotels, Diageo, Organic Basics, Three Spirit, The Malin, among others. Previously she was an Insight Strategist at Protein Agency in Brooklyn, New York.

Her expertise lies in building brand theory built from a human-centered perspective. She creates brand frameworks and narratives that come from in-depth qualitative research to create strategies that are relevant for brands and resonant to consumers’ cultural worlds. Projects often take the form of: consumer segmentations, trend reports, and new-to-market strategies.

Report clips available upon request.
