Sophia Callahan
Strategy

Sophia Callahan is a freelance insight and brand strategist based in the San Francisco Bay Area. She has written for WGSN and worked with various agencies on client projects for Nike, Hydro Flask, Apple, Viacom, Instagram, Feeld, Walton Family Foundation, Dubai Arts & Culture, W Hotels, Diageo, Organic Basics, and Three Spirit, among others.

Projects have taken the form of consumer segmentations, trend reports, new-to-market strategies, and brand audits for strategic repositioning. They have attempted to answer questions like: what is the future of sport? What do young people need today to feel truly alive? What cultural positioning do we need to create a new media museum in a changing landscape? What is the future of hospitality? What are the new values around socializing as alcohol consumption wanes?

Report clips are available upon request.
